Saudi HRDF (Hadaf) Wage Support Program: Executive Guide for HR & CFOs

A comprehensive breakdown of the Employment Support Program (برنامج دعم التوظيف) managed by the Human Resources Development Fund (صندوق تنمية الموارد البشرية - هدف) in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

1 Overview & Purpose of Hadaf Support

To accelerate Saudization and reduce private sector talent acquisition risks, the Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF) provides monthly cash subsidies directly offsetting the salary of eligible Saudi citizens. The initiative targets job seekers entering private entities registered on the national Qiwa and Taqat portals.

The standard framework offers a base support rate of 30% of the employee’s registered monthly wage in Year 1 and Year 2, with targeted demographic escalations increasing the government contribution up to a maximum cap of 50% or SAR 3,000 per month (whichever is lower).

2 Wage Caps & Eligibility Rules

To prevent wage inflation and support baseline equitable employment, HRDF enforces statutory minimums and maximum caps:

  • Minimum Qualifying Wage: The employee’s registered wage in GOSI (Basic + Housing Allowance) must be at least SAR 3,200.
  • Maximum Registered Ceiling: The wage can be up to SAR 15,000 for eligibility consideration.
  • Absolute Monthly Cap: Under no circumstance does the monthly subsidy disburse more than SAR 3,000 per employee.
  • Tenure Limitation: Maximum duration is capped at 24 calendar months per verified employee registration.

3 Mathematical Formula & Calculation Engine

Our tool models the exact compounding criteria used in Qiwa approval matrices:

Base Rate: 30%

Adders (+10% each): Female (+10%), Disability (+10%), SME 1-49 (+10%), Remote Governorate (+10%), Critical Sector (+10%)

Effective Rate: Min(Base Rate + Sum(Adders), 50%)

Monthly Subsidy: Min(Effective Rate × Gross Wage, 3000 SAR)

Employer Net Cost: (Gross Wage + GOSI 11.75%) - Monthly Subsidy

The employer GOSI rate includes Old Age, Disability & Death annuities (9%), Occupational Hazards insurance (2%), and SANED Unemployment Insurance (0.75%), totaling 11.75% for Saudi personnel.

4 Common Disqualification Pitfalls

Saudi SMEs frequently lose HRDF disbursements due to clerical and compliance mistakes:

  • Submitting the support request past the statutory 120-day Qiwa onboarding window.
  • Discrepancy between GOSI registered salary and the Qiwa digital employment contract.
  • Establishment slipping into Red or Low Green Nitaqat Saudization zones.
  • Delinquency in Mudad Wage Protection System (WPS) monthly payroll compliance certifications.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can an employee receive HRDF support more than once? ▼

As per the updated Hadaf regulations, a Saudi citizen who previously benefited from Hadaf support can potentially benefit again if at least 2 years have elapsed since the end of the previous subsidy period, provided their cumulative historical subsidy usage does not exceed the total lifetime allowable threshold (generally 24 months).

Does HRDF disburse payments to the company or the employee directly? ▼

The enterprise pays the full contractual salary to the employee via the Mudad Wage Protection System (WPS) monthly. After payment verification and GOSI confirmation, HRDF deposits the approved subsidy directly into the employer's registered IBAN account on a monthly reimbursement cycle.

How does the subsidy interact with the Saudization (Nitaqat) score? ▼

Subsidized Saudi hires count toward your organization's Saudization quota as long as their registered salary is at or above SAR 4,000 for a full 1.0 Nitaqat credit (wages between SAR 3,000 and 3,999 count as half an employee in Nitaqat metrics).
