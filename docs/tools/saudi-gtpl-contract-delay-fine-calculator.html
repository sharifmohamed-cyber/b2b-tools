Complete Guide to Delay Fines Under Saudi GTPL (Government Tenders & Procurement Law) الدليل الشامل لاحتساب غرامات التأخير في نظام المنافسات والمشتريات الحكومية السعودي الجديد

Under the revised Saudi Government Tenders and Procurement Law (Royal Decree No. M/128, dated 13/11/1440H and its Executive Regulations), public sector contractors in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia face rigorous statutory mechanisms governing project delays. Whether executing infrastructure mega-projects under Vision 2030, municipal roadworks, or technical maintenance contracts, project directors and financial controllers must precisely forecast potential delay liabilities.

Official GTPL Fine Formula (Article 74 & Executive Regulations) معادلة غرامة التأخير الرسمية (المادة 74 واللائحة التنفيذية)

Average Daily Cost = (Adjusted Contract Value) ÷ (Contract Execution Period in Days)
Gross Delay Fine = Delay Days × Average Daily Cost × Tier Rate (or Contract Specific Factor)
Statutory Cap Check = Min(Gross Fine, Adjusted Contract Value × Cap % [6% or 10%])
Total Contractor Deduction = Applied Fine + (Delay Days × Supervision Consultant Daily Cost)

Where the total contractual period accounts for officially approved written extensions (Variation Orders, force majeure, or delays solely attributable to the government contracting entity).

Key Statutory Principles Every KSA Contractor Must Know المبادئ النظامية الأساسية التي يجب على كل مقاول معرفتها

  • The Statutory Cap Ceiling: Delay penalties cannot exceed 10% of the total adjusted contract value for Public Works, Maintenance & Operations, and Consulting contracts. For supply and procurement contracts, the statutory ceiling is strictly capped at 6% of the contract value.
  • Supervision Fees are Additional: Article 74 explicitly mandates that the contractor shall bear the actual supervisory consultant costs incurred during the unexcused delay period. This deduction is calculated separately from and is NOT restricted by the 6% or 10% penalty ceiling.
  • Partial Beneficial Handover: If the government entity takes over completed milestones or distinct parts that can be independently commissioned and utilized, the delay penalty is levied solely against the value of the uncompleted and unusable portion.
  • Extension of Time (EoT) Applications: Delays caused by entity site access restrictions, delayed advance payments, scope increases exceeding statutory boundaries, or third-party utility delays qualify for justified time extensions without fine imposition.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) الأسئلة الشائعة حول الغرامات والتمديد

Can a government entity waive delay fines under Saudi Law? هل يجوز للجهة الحكومية الإعفاء من غرامة التأخير؟

Under GTPL regulations, government entities cannot unilaterally waive accrued statutory fines without obtaining prior approval from the Ministry of Finance or a formal determination by the dedicated Disputes & Violation Committee, unless the delay was due to an approved force majeure event or employer default.

How are delay penalties deducted through the Etimad platform? كيف يتم حسم الغرامات عبر منصة اعتماد الحكومية؟

Payment certificates uploaded via Etimad Financial Claims automatically include contractual deduction lines. The supervising entity inputs the calculated penalty and consultant costs, deducting them directly from the final milestone payment, release of retention, or performance guarantee letters.

What happens if the delay penalty reaches the 10% maximum ceiling? ماذا يحدث إذا بلغت الغرامة الحد الأقصى (10%)؟

Once the 10% ceiling is hit, the government contracting body is legally empowered to issue a notice of default, withdraw the works at the contractor's expense (سحب العمل والتنفيذ على حساب المقاول), or terminate the contract under GTPL Article 76.
