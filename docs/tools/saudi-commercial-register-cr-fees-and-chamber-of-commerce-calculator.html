Complete Guide to Saudi Commercial Register (CR) & Chamber of Commerce Fees

Operating a business in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia requires maintaining active legal standing through the Ministry of Commerce (MoC) and the local Chamber of Commerce. Whether you run a Limited Liability Company (LLC), a branch office, or a Sole Proprietorship (Muassasa), understanding the fee structure is crucial for accurate corporate budgeting and cash flow forecasting.

1. Ministry of Commerce (MoC) Commercial Register Fees

The MoC commercial register fee depends on whether the registration is for a main office or a branch, and the number of years selected for renewal (up to 5 years). Typically, the base fee for a main commercial register is 1,200 SAR per year for corporate entities like LLCs, plus variable fees for branches. Failing to renew on time incurs late penalties under Saudi commercial regulations.

2. Chamber of Commerce Subscription Dues

Chamber of Commerce membership is mandatory for all commercial entities in Saudi Arabia. The subscription fee is calculated based on the company's capital tier and the specific regional chamber (e.g., Riyadh Chamber, Jeddah Chamber, or Eastern Province Chamber). Chambers are categorized into grades (Excellence, 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th) reflecting the company size and capital investment.

3. Formula & Calculation Methodology

Total CR & Chamber Fee = (MoC Annual Fee × Years) + (Chamber Annual Dues × Years) + Electronic Portal Services

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I renew my Commercial Register for more than one year?

A: Yes, the Saudi Ministry of Commerce allows businesses to renew their CRs for a duration ranging from 1 to 5 years, providing convenience and potential alignment with municipal licenses (Balady).

Q: What happens if my CR renewal is delayed?

A: Delays in renewing the commercial register can lead to administrative fines, suspension of governmental e-services (Qiwa, GOSI, Muqeem), and potential legal complications with bank accounts.

Q: Are Chamber of Commerce fees uniform across all regions?

A: No, subscription dues vary slightly depending on the regional chamber (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, etc.) and your established company grading tier.
