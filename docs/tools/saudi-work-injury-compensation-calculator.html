Comprehensive Guide to Work Injury Compensation & GOSI Occupational Hazard Rules in Saudi Arabia

الدليل الشامل لتعويضات إصابات العمل ونظام الأخطار المهنية في المملكة العربية السعودية

In the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, workplace safety, occupational health, and financial security for injured employees are governed strictly by two primary frameworks: the Saudi Labor Law (Articles 133 through 139) and the GOSI Occupational Hazards Branch (فرع الأخطار المهنية). Every registered company operating in Saudi Arabia must comply with mandatory employer-paid contributions and proper reporting procedures whenever a work-related accident, commute injury, or occupational disease occurs.

في المملكة العربية السعودية، تُحكم سلامة بيئة العمل وتوفير الحماية المالية والمادية للموظفين المصابين بموجب نظامين أساسيين: نظام العمل السعودي (المواد من 133 إلى 139) وفرع الأخطار المهنية بالمؤسسة العامة للتأمينات الاجتماعية. يلتزم جميع أصحاب العمل بتسديد الاشتراكات الإلزامية والإبلاغ الدقيق عن أي حادث عمل أو إصابة طريق أو مرض مهني.

📜 Key Saudi Labor Law Articles أهم مواد نظام العمل الخاصة بالإصابات

  • Article 133 (المادة 133): Mandatory provision of immediate medical care, transport, and treatment expenses by the employer or GOSI.
  • Article 137 (المادة 137): Daily allowance equal to 100% of full wage during temporary disability. If treatment exceeds 30 days or is provided under GOSI hospital care, it may adjust to 75%.
  • Article 138 (المادة 138): Evaluation of permanent total or partial disability must be certified by accredited GOSI medical committees.
  • Article 139 (المادة 139): Employer non-compliance or failure to register employees under GOSI makes the employer directly liable for compensation payments.

🧮 Compensation Formulas & Rules معادلات وقواعد التعويضات المعتمدة

  • Temporary Allowance: (Wage / 30) × Days × Rate (100% or 75%)
  • Non-Saudi Permanent Disability (Lump Sum):
    • Total Disability / Death = 84 months wage (Cap: SAR 330,000) + Funeral grant (SAR 10,000).
    • Partial (<50%) = 60 months wage × Disability % (Cap: SAR 165,000).
  • Saudi National Benefits:
    • Total Disability = 100% Monthly Pension (Min SAR 1,750/mo).
    • Partial (≥50%) = Monthly pension proportional to disability percentage.

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Work Injury Incidents

خطوات الإجراءات التشغيلية عند وقوع إصابة عمل

Step 1: Emergency Care (0-3 hrs)

Provide immediate first aid and transfer the worker to a GOSI-accredited medical facility or hospital.

Step 2: Formal Reporting (<72 hrs)

Report the injury electronically via Qiwa / GOSI portal within 72 hours of occurrence to preserve insurance rights.

Step 3: Medical Board & Settlement

Submit medical reports to the GOSI Medical Committee for final assessment of disability percentage and payout execution.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

الأسئلة الشائعة حول إصابات العمل والأخطار المهنية

Q1: Who pays for the GOSI Occupational Hazard contribution rate?

The 2% Occupational Hazard contribution is paid 100% by the employer for both Saudi and non-Saudi employees. No portion of this fee is deducted from the worker's salary.

Q2: Are traffic accidents during commutes covered as work injuries?

Yes. Accidents occurring while commuting directly to or from the workplace, or while traveling on official business trips, are recognized as official work injuries under GOSI rules.

Q3: What happens if an employer fails to report an injury within 72 hours?

Late reporting may lead to administrative fines under Labor Inspection rules and requires the employer to bear full medical expenses out-of-pocket until GOSI accepts the late report.
