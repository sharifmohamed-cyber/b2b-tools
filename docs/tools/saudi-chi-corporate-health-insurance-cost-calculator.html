Comprehensive Guide to Saudi CHI Corporate Health Insurance Rules & Costs

Learn how employee health insurance works under the Saudi Council of Health Insurance regulations.

1. Mandatory CHI Health Insurance Rules in Saudi Arabia

Under the Saudi Cooperative Health Insurance Law, all private sector companies operating in Saudi Arabia are legally required to provide continuous medical insurance coverage for all non-Saudi and Saudi employees, as well as their eligible dependents residing in the Kingdom.

  • Eligible Dependents: Employee's spouse(s), male children up to the age of 25, and unmarried female children.
  • Unified Benefit Policy Limit: Minimum SAR 500,000 annual maximum per insured.
  • Employer Liability: Employers cannot deduct health insurance costs from employee salary.

2. Key Factors Affecting Corporate Health Insurance Premiums

Insurance companies apply actuarial risk formulas based on dynamic parameters:

Age Bracket Distribution

Older age brackets carry higher premium rates.

Network Tier Class

VIP & Class A plans include top tier hospital networks.

Group Size Discount Pool

Larger group sizes benefit from volume discounts.

3. Premium Estimation Formula Breakdown

Base Premium = Σ (Class Employee Count × Age Bracket Rate) + (Dependents Count × Dependent Avg Rate)
Subtotal Premium = Base Premium × (1 + Risk/Pre-existing Loading %) × (1 - Group Size Discount %)
Total Regulatory Fees = Total Insured Lives × CHI Platform Fee (SAR 15)
KSA VAT Amount = (Subtotal Premium + Total Regulatory Fees) × 15%
Final Total Cost = Subtotal Premium + Total Regulatory Fees + KSA VAT Amount

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is VAT applicable to corporate health insurance in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Standard 15% VAT is applied to all commercial insurance policies per ZATCA rules.

What happens if an employee's insurance is not renewed in time?

Failure to maintain coverage leads to heavy fines and Qiwa/Muqeem service blocks.

Can an SME mix different classes for different employee seniority levels?

Yes, employers can assign different coverage tiers based on job roles, provided all tiers meet CHI minimums.
