The Comprehensive Guide to Saudi Aramco's IKTVA Program & Scoring Mechanism

Everything enterprise CFOs, bidding directors, and procurement managers in Saudi Arabia need to know to pass third-party audits and win energy sector contracts.

1. What is the Saudi Aramco IKTVA Program?

The In-Kingdom Total Value Add (IKTVA) program was established by Saudi Aramco in December 2015 to baseline and drive domestic value creation across its entire supply chain ecosystem. Designed in harmony with Saudi Vision 2030, the overarching goal of IKTVA is to achieve 70% localization of supply chain spending while creating high-caliber industrial jobs for Saudi nationals and elevating domestic manufacturing capabilities.

Any enterprise supplying goods, heavy equipment, engineering design, field construction, or professional services to Saudi Aramco—directly as a Tier 1 supplier or indirectly as a sub-contractor—must measure, audit, and systematically improve its IKTVA ratio. An unfavorable score directly penalizes the supplier during commercial tender evaluations, while a high score provides a quantifiable competitive margin.

2. Official IKTVA Mathematical Formula

% IKTVA = [ (A + B + C + D + R) / E ] × 100

Each metric in the numerator and denominator represents a distinct line item validated against audited financial statements, payroll General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) records, and local commercial invoices:

A: Local Goods & Services (SAR)

Expenditures on physical goods manufactured in Saudi Arabia and services procured from vendors holding valid Saudi Commercial Registrations and ZATCA VAT numbers. Imported inventory does not count.

B: Saudi Nationals Payroll (SAR)

Total compensation, including base salaries, housing allowances, transport allowances, and annual bonuses paid strictly to registered Saudi citizen employees during the calendar/fiscal year.

C: Training & Development (SAR)

Direct funds spent on professional credentials, apprenticeships, engineering accreditations, and formal corporate training specialized for Saudi staff.

D: Supplier & SME Incubation (SAR)

Documented investments dedicated to developing local Saudi vendors, upgrading subcontractor quality control procedures, and technical technology transfers.

R: Research & Development (SAR)

Capital and operational disbursements towards applied in-Kingdom R&D, local industrial testing, patents filed with SAIP, and university joint research.

E: Total KSA Revenue (SAR)

Total gross revenue recognized from Saudi Arabian operations. This serves as the benchmark denominator scaling your local spend into an absolute percentage.

3. How to Pass the Aramco Authorized IKTVA Audit

To officially submit your IKTVA survey to Saudi Aramco, your raw data must be audited and stamped by one of the authorized Independent Third-Party Audit Firms (such as PwC, KPMG, Deloitte, EY, BDO, or Baker Tilly). Follow these core steps to ensure compliance without qualification disclaimers:

  • Reconciliation with Zakat Submissions: Revenue (E) must match the gross operating revenue declared to the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA).
  • GOSI Certification: Total Saudi compensation (B) must reconcile with monthly GOSI Wage Protection System (WPS) filings, net of non-Saudi allowances.
  • Local Vendor Proof: All local spend (A) must show invoices displaying a valid Saudi Tax Identification Number (TIN) and verified in-Kingdom physical presence.
  • 5-Year 70% Improvement Plan: Companies bidding on mega-contracts are required to present an official IKTVA 5-Year Headway Plan outlining how they will reach 70% through local hiring, training, and sourcing.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can the calculated IKTVA score exceed 100%? ▼

Yes. In growth phases, capital expenditure (CAPEX) on localized manufacturing assets, combined with aggressive workforce training and high local sourcing, can cause total domestic spend to exceed annual recognized revenue (E). Aramco surveys accommodate scores above 100%, though auditors will perform thorough validation tests.

What is the difference between IKTVA and the LCGPA Local Content Score? ▼

IKTVA is Saudi Aramco's proprietary value creation formula specifically designed for its energy and petrochemical supply chains. The Local Content and Government Procurement Authority (LCGPA) governs government ministry tenders and applies a broader baseline across all public sectors. While the underlying components (Salaries, Goods, Training, Depreciation) are conceptually aligned, their weightings and formal reporting portals differ.

How does a higher IKTVA score impact Aramco tender evaluations? ▼

During commercial bidding, Aramco calculates an 'Evaluated Price' by applying an IKTVA weighting factor against the vendor's raw financial quote. A vendor with a 75% score receives a favorable evaluation pricing adjustment compared to a competitor offering the same quote with a 25% score, frequently tipping multi-million dollar contract awards.
