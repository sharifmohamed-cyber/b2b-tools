Complete Guide to Saudi Qiwa, GOSI & Mudad Compliance in 2025

Operating a business in Saudi Arabia requires seamless alignment across three major governmental and semi-governmental platforms: Qiwa (Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development), GOSI (General Organization for Social Insurance), and Mudad (Wage Protection System). Failure to synchronize data across these portals results in heavy administrative fines, suspension of government services, and Nitaqat rating degradation.

1. Qiwa Platform: Contract Digitization & Saudization

Under MHRSD regulations, all employment contracts for both Saudi nationals and expatriates must be documented and digitally authenticated through the Qiwa platform. Unauthenticated contracts or discrepancies between registered salaries and actual payouts lead to immediate penalties and compromise the company's Saudization (Nitaqat) score.

2. GOSI Contribution & Wage Alignment

GOSI contributions are calculated based on the employee's basic wage plus housing allowance. Employers must ensure that the wage reported on GOSI perfectly matches the salary transferred through Mudad SIF files. Any significant mismatch triggers automated audit flags.

3. Mudad Wage Protection System (WPS) Deadlines

Mudad monitors timely wage payments. Salaries must be disbursed through certified WPS channels within agreed timelines. Delays exceeding statutory grace periods incur tiered fines starting from SAR 3,000 up to company-wide service suspensions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if GOSI wages differ from Mudad WPS transfers?

Discrepancies invite labor inspectors to request payroll justification. It can lead to retroactive social insurance assessments and penalties.

How does Qiwa affect work visa quotas?

Qiwa directly links your active verified contracts and Nitaqat tier to your ability to issue new visas, renew Iqamas, or modify professional titles.
