Published for GCC HR Directors, General Managers, CFOs, and Qiwa Compliance Officers. موجّه لمدراء الموارد البشرية، المدراء الماليين ومسؤولي التوافق في المنشآت.

1. The Core Nitaqat Calculation Methodology ١. آلية ومعادلة احتساب نطاقات الدقيقة

Under the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MoHR), the Saudization percentage is no longer a simple headcount fraction. The rate is evaluated across an establishment’s unified group (الرقم الموحد) over a 26-week rolling arithmetic mean. Different categories of national employees contribute distinct point weights based on their registered GOSI base wage and physical capabilities:

Official Weighted Headcount Formula:
Weighted Saudis = (S_full × 1.0) + (S_partial × 0.5) + (S_pwd × 4.0*) + (S_flexible × 0.5)
Saudization Percentage (%) = [ Weighted Saudis / (Weighted Saudis + Total Expats) ] × 100
*Note: Disabled Saudi employees (PwD) count as 4 units, but cannot exceed a maximum ceiling of 10% of total Saudis in the entity under standard regulations.

2. Nitaqat Color Bands and Operational Implications ٢. نطاقات الألوان والآثار التشغيلية على التأشيرات ورخص العمل

An establishment’s tier directly controls its operational viability in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Dropping into lower tiers restricts essential government services:

Platinum (البلاتيني)

Top 20% performance bracket in the sector. Unrestricted visa allocation via Qiwa, instant change of professions, ability to hire expatriates from Red or Low Green firms without release letters, and six-month grace periods on minor infractions.

High Green (الأخضر المرتفع)

Standard high-compliance bracket. Provides visa requests (subject to quota balances), uninterrupted work permit (Iqama) renewals for up to 2 years, and transfer of employee sponsorships into the entity.

Mid Green (الأخضر المتوسط)

Operational threshold. Permits Iqama renewals and internal transfers. However, new block visa applications are heavily restricted or throttled until Saudization is elevated.

Low Green (الأخضر المنخفض)

Warning buffer. Businesses can renew work permits for existing personnel but are barred from obtaining new visas or transferring in additional expatriate staff.

Red Zone (النطاق الأحمر) - Immediate Risk

Non-compliant tier. Expatriate staff are legally permitted to transfer their sponsorships to other compliant entities without employer consent or notice. Blocked from opening new branch files, renewing work permits, or acquiring commercial licensing extensions.

3. GOSI Wage Rules for Saudization Credit ٣. شروط الأجور المحتسبة في التأمينات الاجتماعية (GOSI)

Under MoHR Ministerial Decision No. 224, minimum wage floors dictate employee weighting in nationalization quotas:

  • Minimum 4,000 SAR monthly salary: Required for a Saudi employee to count as one full point (1.0) in Nitaqat.
  • Between 3,000 SAR and 3,999 SAR: The employee is counted as exactly one half (0.5) point.
  • Below 3,000 SAR: The employee is not counted toward the Saudization ratio whatsoever, despite remaining registered on GOSI.
  • Part-time Contracts: Registered via Qiwa’s flexible/part-time scheme count as 0.5 points, provided GOSI contributions meet the pro-rated threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions (HR & Finance Teams) الأسئلة الشائعة لمدراء الموارد البشرية والمالية

How fast does hiring a Saudi reflect on our Qiwa status?

Qiwa employs a rolling 26-week cumulative calculation. When you register a new Saudi on GOSI, the system adds 1/26th of their weight in week one, reaching full 1.0 weight by week 26. Instant status jump is mitigated by this rolling average to prevent seasonal hiring manipulation. Fast-track provisions apply to startups in their first year.

Can one company branch be Platinum while another is Red?

No. Nitaqat calculations aggregate all branches operating under the Unified Commercial Number (الرقم الموحد). A failure in one subsidiary or branch directly drags down the collective group rating for the entire organization.

What is the maximum quota for counting special categories?

For employees with disabilities (PwD) counted as 4 points, the law limits this concession to not exceed 10% of the total Saudi workforce count within the establishment. Any excess disabled employees beyond that ceiling count as standard 1.0 employees.
