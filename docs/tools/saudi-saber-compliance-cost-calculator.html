Comprehensive Guide to SABER & SASO Certification in Saudi Arabia

The SABER system is an electronic platform launched by the Saudi Standards, Metrology and Quality Organization (SASO) in partnership with THIQAH Business Services. It is designed to facilitate the online issuance of Certificate of Conformity (CoC) for products imported into Saudi Arabia under the Saudi Product Safety Program (SALEEM). Understanding the fee structures, risk categories, and certificate requirements is critical for supply chain managers, customs brokers, and enterprise trade operations in the Kingdom.

1. Product Certificate of Conformity (PCoC)

  • Official SASO Platform Fee: SAR 500 (+ 15% VAT) per model/product line.
  • Validity: 1 Calendar Year from issuance date.
  • CB Service Fee: Negotiated with private accredited audit bodies (typically SAR 1,200 to SAR 4,500 based on complexity).

2. Shipment Certificate of Conformity (SCoC)

  • Official SASO Platform Fee: SAR 350 (+ 15% VAT) per shipment batch.
  • Validity: Specific to the bill of lading / single consignment.
  • Low Risk Products: Require Self-Declaration PCoC (SAR 500) and SCoC (SAR 350).

SABER Annual Budget Calculation Formula

Total Annual Net Cost = [ NPCoC × ( FeeSASO_PCoC + FeeCB + FeeTesting ) ] + [ NSCoC × FeeSASO_SCoC ] + FeeAudit

VAT (15%) = Total Annual Net Cost × 0.15

Grand Total Gross Cost = Total Annual Net Cost + VAT (15%)

Where: FeeSASO_PCoC = 500 SAR, FeeSASO_SCoC = 350 SAR, and N represents the counts of unique regulated products and annual shipment volume.
