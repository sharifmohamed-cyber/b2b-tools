Understanding Saudi Qiwa Employee Transfer & Sponsorship Fees

Transferring an employee under the Saudi Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) via the Qiwa platform involves multiple structured statutory fees. These include the official Qiwa transfer fee, Jawazat (Passport Directorate) Iqama renewal or transfer stamping fees, expatriate levies (Muqeem / Lavies), and dependent fees. Managing these expenses accurately is critical for human resources budgets and onboarding planning in Saudi Arabia.

How Qiwa Transfer Fees Are Calculated

Under current regulations, the Qiwa transfer fee escalates depending on how many times the employee has previously transferred sponsors within the Kingdom:

  • First Transfer: SAR 2,000 payable by the new employer.
  • Second Transfer: SAR 4,000 payable by the new employer.
  • Third Transfer or More: SAR 6,000 payable by the new employer.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Who is legally responsible for paying the Qiwa transfer fee?

By law and standard practice, the hiring (new) employer bears the official Qiwa transfer and government sponsorship fees. Deducting these fees from the employee's salary is strictly prohibited under Saudi Labor Law.

Can an employee transfer without the current employer's approval?

Under the Labor Reform Initiative (LRI), employees can transfer upon contract expiration, during the notice period, or if specific conditions like unpaid wages or absence of a documented contract are met without requiring explicit consent via Qiwa.
