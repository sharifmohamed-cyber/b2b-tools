Complete Guide to CSV Attendance & Payroll Automation

Processing monthly payroll from raw biometric attendance machine exports can be tedious and prone to human calculation errors. Whether your workforce uses ZKTeco, Hikvision, or manual punch logs, converting CSV attendance exports correctly ensures legal compliance, boosts employee trust, and eliminates friction in finance departments.

Standard Payroll Calculation Formula

Net Payable = (Regular Hours * Hourly Rate) + (Overtime Hours * Hourly Rate * OT Multiplier) - (Late Minutes / 60 * Penalty Rate)

This formula accounts for standard productivity hours while fairly compensating extra effort and penalizing consistent tardiness based on company HR policy regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I import my biometric machine CSV directly?

Most attendance machines export CSV files containing Employee ID, Punch Timestamp, and Status. You can map columns or paste summarized totals directly into our web converter tool.

How is overtime calculated under standard labor laws?

Typically, hours worked beyond standard daily or weekly hours are multiplied by 1.25x to 1.5x of the normal hourly wage rate.

Is my employee attendance data secure?

Yes! All calculations happen locally within your browser via JavaScript. No sensitive employee data is ever stored or transmitted to external servers.
