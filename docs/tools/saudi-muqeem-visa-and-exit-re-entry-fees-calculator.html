Complete Guide to Saudi Muqeem, Exit Re-Entry, and Iqama Fees

Managing government relations (PRO) functions in Saudi Arabia requires accurate budgeting for employee Iqama renewals, dependent levies, and exit re-entry (Khurooj Oadah) visas. Under the regulations administered by the General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat) and accessed via Muqeem or Absher Business portals, fee schedules depend strictly on visa type, duration, and dependent status.

Official Fee Schedule Breakdown

  • Single Exit Re-Entry Visa: 200 SAR for up to 2 months, plus 100 SAR for each additional month.
  • Multiple Exit Re-Entry Visa: 500 SAR for 3 months, plus 200 SAR for each additional month.
  • Final Exit Visa (Khurooj Nihai): Free of charge, provided valid Iqama and no pending traffic fines or violations.
  • Iqama Renewal: Varies by profession category (e.g., 650 SAR for corporate employees, plus dependent expatriate levies where applicable).

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: Can exit re-entry visas be refunded if unused?

Yes, unused exit re-entry visas can be cancelled through Absher or Muqeem before expiry to receive a fee refund credited to the employer's or employee's government account.

Q2: Are traffic violations checked before issuing visas?

Yes, the system automatically blocks the issuance of exit re-entry or final exit visas if the sponsor or sponsored individual has unpaid traffic violations.
