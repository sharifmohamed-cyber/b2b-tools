Comprehensive Guide to Saudi Withholding Tax (WHT) Regulations

Under the Saudi Income Tax Law enacted by Royal Decree No. (M/1) and overseen by the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA), any resident entity or permanent establishment (PE) in the Kingdom making payments to non-resident entities without a permanent establishment in Saudi Arabia is legally mandated to deduct Withholding Tax (WHT) at source. Withholding tax ensures that cross-border economic activity generated within Saudi Arabia contributes equitably to the state treasury.

Understanding the Gross-Up Formula in International Procurement

International vendors frequently demand contractual clauses stipulating that invoices must be received "Net of any local taxes, levies, or source withholdings". In such cases, the Saudi payor cannot simply subtract WHT from the vendor's agreed invoice balance. Instead, the company must assume the tax as an additional financial burden through a process called Grossing-Up.

Standard Gross-Up Equation:

Gross Base Amount = Net Contract Amount ÷ (1 - Tax Rate)

Withholding Tax Amount to ZATCA:

WHT Liability = Gross Base Amount × Tax Rate

Numerical Example: Suppose an overseas technology firm invoices USD $100,000 for SaaS licensing (15% WHT) on a net contract basis. The taxable base becomes $100,000 ÷ (1 - 0.15) = $117,647.06. The Saudi company remits $100,000.00 to the vendor and pays $17,647.06 to ZATCA. The true economic surcharge on your enterprise is 17.65%.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

When is the exact deadline to remit Withholding Tax in Saudi Arabia?

According to Article 68 of the Income Tax Regulations, Withholding Tax must be filed through the ZATCA portal and paid within the first 10 days of the calendar month following the month in which the foreign payment or realization of income occurred.

Can Double Taxation Treaties (DTAA) eliminate the Withholding Tax?

Yes. Saudi Arabia maintains comprehensive Double Tax Avoidance Agreements with more than 55 jurisdictions (including the UAE, UK, France, Germany, Japan, Singapore, and China). Under many treaties, technical and business service fees are classified under "Business Profits" (Article 7) and taxed at 0% in KSA unless the non-resident maintains a permanent establishment. However, a Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) and Form Q7B must be collected before applying treaty rates.

Does Withholding Tax apply in addition to Value Added Tax (VAT)?

Yes. WHT is a direct income tax withholding, whereas VAT is a consumption tax. Under the Saudi VAT Law, cross-border services received by a taxable Saudi business are subject to the Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM) at 15%. Businesses record both the input and output tax simultaneously on Box 9 of their VAT returns.
