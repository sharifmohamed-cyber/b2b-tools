Key Requirements for Balady Permits

To secure a building permit in Saudi Arabia, property owners must contract with an accredited engineering office (مكتب هندسي معتمد), obtain soil investigation reports, ensure full compliance with the Saudi Building Code (SBC 2018/2021), and clear all Balady portal electronic review steps.

Understanding Saudi Building Code (SBC) & Balady Permit Fees

Navigating real estate development and construction in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia requires strict adherence to the Saudi Building Code (SBC) and municipal regulations managed through the Balady platform (بلدي). Whether you are constructing a private residential villa, a commercial retail complex, or an industrial warehouse, estimating fees accurately is critical for capital budgeting.

How Balady Municipal Permit Fees are Calculated

Balady permit fees depend heavily on the property classification, total built-up area (BUA in square meters), and the municipality category of the city. Major metropolitan areas such as Riyadh, Jeddah, and Dammam fall under Category A, where service standards and application workflows are fully digitized through accredited engineering offices.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is included in the Saudi Building Code (SBC) compliance?

SBC covers structural integrity, electrical wiring, mechanical ventilation, fire safety, energy conservation (SBC 601/602), and green building standards.

2. Do I need an engineering office for a building permit?

Yes, all construction, addition, and major renovation permits in Saudi Arabia must be submitted through an approved engineering consulting office registered on the Balady portal.

3. Are VAT and municipal taxes applied to construction permits?

Official government permit fees are generally exempt from VAT, but services provided by engineering offices, contractors, and material suppliers are subject to the standard 15% ZATCA VAT rate.
