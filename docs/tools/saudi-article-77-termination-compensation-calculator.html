Comprehensive Legal Guide: Article 77 of the Saudi Labor Law

Article 77 represents one of the most commercially critical and frequently litigated statutes in Saudi Arabia's labor and employment jurisprudence. Reformed to create a predictable financial mechanism for terminating employment contracts when valid justifiable cause (as restricted by Article 80) is absent, Article 77 establishes statutory floors, default metrics, and limits of financial compensation for both employers and employees.

01

Indefinite-Term Contracts

Applicable principally to Saudi nationals (non-Saudis cannot hold indefinite contracts under Article 37). Unless a different higher or lower compensation is specified in the employment agreement, compensation equals 15 days of the employee's total gross wage for each completed year of service.

02

Fixed-Term Contracts

Mandatory for expatriates and agreed fixed durations. If terminated unilaterally without lawful cause prior to expiration, the aggrieved party is entitled to the full wage for the remaining duration of the contractual period, unless the agreement explicitly specifies an alternative compensation clause.

03

Statutory Minimum Floor (2 Months)

Crucially, Article 77 establishes an immutable statutory threshold: the compensation awarded under paragraphs (1) or (2) must not be less than two months' full wages, even if the tenure is just 6 months or the remaining contract duration is only 15 days.

The Article 77 Settlement Calculation Engine: Formula Breakdown

A legally compliant separation settlement involves four separate components that must not be conflated:

Total Company Liability = Article 77 Indemnity + Pay in Lieu of Notice (Articles 75 & 76) + Unused Accrued Vacation Encashment (Article 111) + Statutory End of Service Gratuity (Article 84)
Where Wage Base = Basic Wage + Housing Allowance + Transportation Allowance + Fixed Guaranteed Allowances.

1. Article 77 Indemnity Computation

  • Fixed-Term Default: Max( Remaining Months × Gross Wage , 2 × Gross Wage )
  • Indefinite Default: Max( (Total Tenure in Years) × (0.5 × Gross Wage) , 2 × Gross Wage )
  • Contractual Stipulation Exception: If the signed contract contains an agreed compensation figure (e.g., 3 months' wage or a set lump sum), that agreed amount supersedes statutory defaults provided it respects labor judicial standards.

2. Notice Period in Lieu (Articles 75 & 76)

For indefinite contracts terminated by the employer, Article 75 mandates a minimum written notice of 60 days before separation. For fixed contracts or when specified, at least 30 days is standard. Failure to let the employee work through the notice period obliges the employer to pay the employee an indemnity equivalent to the worker’s wage for the duration of the unserved notice period (Article 76).

3. Involuntary EOSB Statutory Gratuity (Article 84)

Unlike employee resignation (Article 85), where EOSB is tiered (0% for under 2 years, 33.3% for 2-5 years, 66.6% for 5-10 years), involuntary employer-initiated termination triggers 100% full entitlement under Article 84 from day one:

  • First 5 years of service: Half-month's gross wage for each completed year.
  • Subsequent years (Year 6+): One full month's gross wage for each completed year.
  • Prorated fractions of a year calculated on exact fractional days.

Frequently Asked Questions (HR & Legal Counsel)

Can an employer fire an employee under Article 77 without any justification? ↓

Yes, Article 77 specifically addresses termination "for an invalid / non-lawful cause". While the termination takes effect and terminates the contractual relationship, it legally obligates the terminating party to pay the strict financial penalties calculated above. However, if the termination is discriminatory or retaliatory (e.g. pregnancy, filing a wage complaint), labor courts may order back-pay and special compensation.

Is Article 77 compensation calculated based on Basic Salary or Total Gross Salary? ↓

Under the prevailing precedents of the Saudi Labor Courts and Article 2 of the Saudi Labor Law, "Wage" (الأجر) unless specifically restricted means the actual total wage, including housing allowances, transportation, and regular commissions. Therefore, Article 77 calculations must incorporate basic salary plus all steady allowances.

Can the employment contract specify 1 month wage instead of the 2-month statutory minimum? ↓

No. Labor Law Article 8 establishes that any contractual stipulation that contradicts the statutory protections of the law or provides less than the worker's minimum statutory right is void ab initio. The two months' gross wage minimum floor is an absolute legal mandate that cannot be waived or contracted out of.

Does paying Article 77 compensation exempt the employer from paying End of Service Gratuity (EOSB)? ↓

No. Article 77 indemnity is damages for the untimely or wrongful breach of the contract duration. It is completely independent of the employee's vested statutory rights under Article 84 (End of Service Gratuity) and Article 111 (Accrued Annual Leave balance). Both must be paid alongside Article 77.
