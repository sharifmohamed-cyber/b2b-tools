1. Legal Framework of Maktab Amal (Qiwa) Work Permit Levies

Under the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) regulations implemented via the Qiwa platform, corporate employers pay a monthly financial compensation levy (المقابل المالي لرخص العمل) for each non-Saudi employee. The fee structure is designed around Saudization (Nitaqat) tiers:

  • Exceeding Saudis (800 SAR/month = 9,600 SAR/year): Applies to expat headcount that exceeds the total count of full-time Saudi citizens employed in the company.
  • Equal to or Fewer than Saudis (700 SAR/month = 8,400 SAR/year): Discounted tier granted to the portion of non-Saudi headcount that is equal to or less than the Saudi staff count.
  • Micro-Enterprise Exemption: Small enterprises with 9 or fewer employees where the full-time Saudi owner is registered under GOSI are eligible for exemption on up to 2-4 expatriate workers according to specific MHRSD criteria.
  • Qiwa Renewal Duration: Employers can choose 3-month, 6-month, 9-month, or 12-month renewal windows to optimize working capital.

2. Directorate General of Passports (Jawazat) Iqama Fees

The Jawazat fee (رسوم رخصة الإقامة) is a distinct statutory cost managed through the Ministry of Interior via Absher Business or Muqeem portals:

  • Corporate / Commercial Expat: 650 SAR per calendar year (500 SAR for Iqama renewal + 150 SAR Human Resources Development / technical levy). For quarterly renewal, this is calculated proportionally at 162.50 SAR per quarter.
  • Domestic Workers (عمالة منزلية): 600 SAR per annum for first-tier domestic workers under individual sponsorship.
  • Agricultural / Livestock: 650 SAR annual government fee.
  • Late Penalty: If an Iqama is not renewed within 3 days of expiration, Jawazat imposes a 500 SAR fine for the 1st violation, and 1,000 SAR for repeated non-compliance.

Total Iqama Period Cost = (Work Permit Levy × Months / 12) + (Jawazat Fee × Months / 12) + (Dependents × 400 SAR × Months) + (Insurance Premium × Months / 12) + (GOSI Hazard 2% × Monthly Salary × Months)

  • Work Permit (Qiwa): 800 × 12 = 9,600 SAR

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) الأسئلة الأكثر تداولاً عن رسوم الإقامات ورخص العمل

Can Saudi SMEs renew Iqamas quarterly (every 3 months)?

Yes. In line with the Saudi Council of Ministers decision, employers and sponsors can issue and renew residency permits (Iqama) and work licenses on a 3, 6, 9, or 12-month basis (with the exclusion of domestic workers). This dramatically aids corporate cash-flow management by eliminating large annual lump-sum outlays.

Who is legally responsible for paying the Expat Dependent Levy (المقابل المالي للمرافقين)?

Under statutory labor standards, the dependent fee is the personal liability of the expatriate employee unless their contractual employment offer or company internal HR policy explicitly provides dependent allowance coverage as an executive benefit.

What is the GOSI Occupational Hazard contribution for expatriates?

In Saudi Arabia, non-Saudi employees do not contribute to standard retirement pensions under GOSI, but employers are strictly mandated to contribute 2.0% of the employee's basic wage and housing allowance for Occupational Hazards (Saned/Annuities do not apply to expats).

What happens if an Iqama expires before renewal?

Employers are granted a 3-day grace period following expiration. Thereafter, the Ministry of Interior imposes an automatic fine of 500 SAR for the first delay, and 1,000 SAR for subsequent delays. In addition, labor services on Qiwa, bank accounts, and health insurance validation are instantly suspended until the renewal fee and fine are cleared.
